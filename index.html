KOSTAS Lab: Knowledge- Oriented Systems for Technology, AI and Science

An independent research lab building machine learning that holds up outside the lab — in IoT networks, in Turkish language data and in the buildings we move through.

Browse publications Meet the team

What we work on

We are two researchers who trained at Edinburgh and now work in Türkiye. Most machine-learning results look strong on the dataset they were built on and fall apart on the next one; our shared interest is in models that generalise — to new devices, new networks, new speakers and new buildings.

We publish our code with our papers, test our methods on data we did not design them for, and care about methods that work for Turkish as well as English.

KOSTAS Lab was founded in 2026. The publications and tools listed here were produced earlier by its members with their co-authors at Heriot-Watt University, the University of Edinburgh, Huawei UK and other institutions; each one is labelled with where the work was done.

KOSTAS Lab is an independent research group run by its two founders. It is not a legal entity and not a unit of any university or public institution; each member's institutional affiliation is listed on their profile.

Research themes

Five lines of work. Most build on research we did before founding the lab; each links to its papers.

  • Generalisable IoT security

    Identifying devices and detecting attacks from network behaviour, with models that keep working on devices, datasets and network conditions they were not trained on. Builds on IoTDevID, IoTGeM and GeMID, developed during Kahraman's PhD at Heriot-Watt University with Dr. Mike Just and Dr. Michael A. Lones.

    device identification, intrusion detection, feature generalisation, network forensics

    6 papers

  • Turkish NLP and offline LLMs

    Evaluating what locally run language models can and cannot do in Turkish, from everyday questions to logical fallacy detection, with co-authors Edibe Yılmaz, İnanç Feridun Canci and Zeynep Gizem Emir Shahbazov. Related student work includes TurkCap, a TÜBİTAK 2209-A project at Gümüşhane University.

    LLM evaluation, reasoning, fallacy detection, image captioning

    3 papers

  • Cognitive language analytics

    Computational analysis of semantic verbal fluency (automatically finding clusters and switches in the words people produce) to support cognitive and clinical assessment for Turkish speakers. Builds on Rabia's PhD at the University of Edinburgh and work with Dr. Maria K. Wolters and Dr. Sarah E. MacPherson.

    verbal fluency, semantic similarity, explainable AI, HCI

    2 papers

  • Indoor positioning

    Locating people and devices where GPS fails, using only WiFi signals. Started with WiFi distance estimation alongside Huawei UK's indoor positioning team (IPIN 2022) and continued with the founders' graph-based floor separation (IPIN 2025).

    WiFi fingerprinting, graph embeddings, floor detection

    2 papers

  • AI for materials and nanobiotechnology

    Interdisciplinary book chapters with Pinar Bozbeyoglu, Elif Esra Altuner and Muzaffer Elmas on AI-driven design of nanocomposites, targeted antibiotic delivery and fuel desulfurisation.

    inverse design, nanocomposites, antimicrobial resistance

    3 papers
