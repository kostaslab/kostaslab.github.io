About

Rabia completed her PhD in Informatics at the University of Edinburgh's School of Informatics (2018–2024). Her thesis, “Automatic Detection of Clusters and Switches in Turkish Semantic Verbal Fluency Data”, develops computational methods for analysing how speakers group and switch between semantic categories — a task widely used in cognitive and clinical assessment.

Her work sits between natural language processing, machine learning and cognitive psychology, and extends to human-computer interaction, indoor positioning and AI-driven materials research. She was a research intern at Huawei UK's Edinburgh R&D centre (2022) and a teaching assistant at the University of Edinburgh.

Since October 2024 she has been a lecturer in the Department of Computer Technologies at Gümüşhane University, where she teaches web programming, systems analysis and artificial intelligence, and supervises TÜBİTAK 2209-A undergraduate research projects. Before her graduate studies she taught computing for five years at the Turkish Ministry of National Education.
