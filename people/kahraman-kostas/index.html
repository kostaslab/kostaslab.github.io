About

Kahraman holds a PhD in Computer Science from Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh. His thesis, “Behaviour-based Security with Machine Learning on IoT Networks” (supervised by Dr. Mike Just and Dr. Michael Lones), showed how conventional feature representations hinder model generalisation, and introduced the IoTDevID, GeMID and IoTGeM frameworks for device identification and attack detection that stay robust across unseen devices and changing network conditions.

During his PhD he worked as a researcher at the Huawei UK R&D Centre in Edinburgh, developing ML solutions for community detection and WiFi-based distance estimation; his team won 1st place in the Huawei UK University Challenge 2021 for indoor localisation.

Since September 2024 he has served as a National Education Expert at the Directorate General of Higher Education and Overseas Education (YYEGM), where he is technical lead and architect of the “Ülkem Yanımda” platform, connecting 100+ teachers with 2,000+ students in 10+ countries. He also holds an MSc in Computer Networks and Security (Distinction) from the University of Essex, and degrees in Computer Education (Marmara University) and Literature.
